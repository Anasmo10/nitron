# Hooksmith Context

## Creator
Nitron Fitness Store (nitron.sport) — the presenter films in the store, talking to camera, with the products on the shelf behind him.

## Niche
Gym accessories that solve one training problem each: lifting straps (figure-8), wrist wraps (Everlast), gloves, resistance bands, hand grippers, belts.

## Viewer
Men and women in Syria, 18–35, lifting in a commercial gym for 6 months to 2 years. They buy accessories because others wear them, but don't know when each one is needed or how to wear it. They watch on the phone between sets or late at night. When a video helps them, they send it to their training partner.
- Pain: wrist hurts on bench; grip gives out before the back on pulls; spent money on gear they never use.
- Desire: lift heavier without the small thing (grip, wrist) stopping them; look like they know what they're doing.

## Proof Allowed
- What the presenter shows on camera (wearing the product, the bar position, opening his fingers with a figure-8 strap).
- IPF rules: wrist wraps max 1 m × 8 cm; the thumb loop must be off the thumb during the lift.
- Figure-8 straps have no quick release, so not for cleans/snatches.
- No sales figures, customer counts, testimonials or injury statistics yet — none may be invented.

## Voice
Syrian Arabic, like a friend who coaches you at the gym: direct, short sentences, no Modern Standard Arabic, no hype. Words he uses: «مو»، «بس»، «هلق»، «يضل»، «بيحمي».

## Formats
Talking head in the store (9:16, lav mic) + product B-roll (black marble and purple light, as in the brand photos). 15–40 s Reels. Brand colours: black, white, purple #76028A.

## CTA Rules
- One CTA at most, and never a sales pitch at the end.
- Allowed: send it to a training partner; comment a keyword to get sizes/price in DM (only if someone will actually reply in DM); save it for the next session.
- No fake urgency, no «لفترة محدودة» unless there is a real offer.

## Forbidden Claims
- Medical promises: «بيمنع الإصابة»، «بيعالج وجع الرسغ».
- «الأفضل بالسوق» or any comparison without proof; no attacking other stores or brands.
- Invented numbers of any kind (views, customers, percentages, weights lifted).
- English gym jargon without saying it the way Syrian gym-goers say it.

## Winning Patterns
- «نحنا بنبيع هالغراض… وأنا بقلك لا تشتري ولا واحد منهن. إلا بحالة وحدة.» — a store telling you not to buy breaks the expectation; the «إلا بحالة وحدة» loop closes at the end.
- «خفّفت الوزن بالبنش… ولسا رسغك بيوجعك؟ لأنو المشكلة مو بالوزن.» — names a situation they lived, then rejects the obvious cause.
- «أصابعي مفتوحة… والبار لسا بإيدي.» — a shot that looks impossible until the product explains it.
- Feedback that rejected batch 1: generic claims, no hook, filler lines — never again.
