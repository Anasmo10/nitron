# facts.md — every number or name on screen, and where it comes from
- 160.6 kg vs 177.0 kg 1RM without vs with straps (+10%), 20 resistance-trained men — Jukic, García-Ramos, Malecek, Omcirk & Tufano, J Strength Cond Res 2020;34(12):3331–3337.
- Greater grip fatigue, lower perceived grip security without straps; 16 men — Jukic et al., Physiology & Behavior 2021;229:113283.
- Deadlift distal-biceps ruptures all in the supinated arm (mixed grip) — Kapicioglu et al., Orthop J Sports Med 2021;9(3) (YouTube video analysis).
- 96.4% of 56 filmed distal-biceps ruptures: forearm supinated, elbow extended — Lappen et al., BMC Musculoskelet Disord 2022.
- L4/L5 compression up to 17,192 N (average estimate, national-level powerlifters, linked-segment model) — Cholewicki, McGill & Norman, Med Sci Sports Exerc 1991. 17,192 N / 9.81 ≈ 1,750 kg-force ≈ 1.7 t (arithmetic).
- Forceful and repetitive activities associated with medial epicondylitis — Shiri et al., Am J Epidemiol 2006;164(11):1065–74 (population study, Finland).
- Muscle and tendon adapt at different rates; the imbalance is discussed as a tendinopathy risk factor — Mersmann, Bohm & Arampatzis, Front Physiol 2017;8:987 (review). Chart = concept sketch, not data.
- Moment = weight × horizontal distance (physics, no study): bar twice as far → twice the moment at the hip/low back.
- Meter capacities in scene 2 (legs/back/grip) are illustrative; only the 160.6 / 177.0 numbers are data.
