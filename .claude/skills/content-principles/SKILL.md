# Content principles

Audience: شباب وصبايا بسوريا بين 18 و35 سنة، بيتمرنوا حديد بنادي من 6 أشهر لسنتين. بيشتروا إكسسوارات
(ستراب، رباط معصم، كفوف، حزام) بس أغلبهن ما بيعرفوا إمتى بتلزم وكيف بتنلبس صح. بيتفرجوا عالموبايل
بين الجولات أو بآخر الليل، وأكتر شي بيعملوه بفيديو عجبهن: بيبعتوه لرفيق التمرين.
Language and tone: عربي لهجة سورية — متل مدرّب صاحبك بالنادي: مباشر، بلا فصحى وبلا مبالغة.

## Principles (each with its reason)
1. Line one sells line two. Reason: nobody keeps watching without a reason in the first seconds.
2. A precise detail beats a general claim. Reason: specifics make it believable.
3. Open a question early and answer it at the end. Reason: an open question keeps people watching.
4. One idea per script. Reason: two ideas dilute both.
5. Start from a situation people live. Reason: they recognise themselves before they hear the information.
6. End on the payoff, not on filler.
7. Every video leaves the viewer one thing to try in their next workout. Reason: this audience trains
   3–5 times a week; a video they can test gets saved and sent to a training partner, and sends carry it
   to people who don't follow us yet.
8. The product appears as the tool that fixes the problem, in the middle — never as an ad at the end.
   Reason: this audience scrolls past ads in the first second, but accepts a tool once they've seen the
   problem it solves.

## How to use
- Before writing, state which principles the script will lean on.
- After writing, check every line against the list and fix what fails.
- Never copy the structure of a reference post. Take only the reason it works.
- Any rule, number or name on screen needs a source (federation rulebook, the product itself, or what the
  presenter says on camera). No source → say it in general terms instead.
